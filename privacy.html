Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Shorts by Me respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Shorts by Me application and its related services.

1. Information We May Access

Depending on the features you use, Shorts by Me may access information provided by connected social media platforms, such as your public profile information, account identifier, and information required to publish or manage content.

2. TikTok Information

If you connect a TikTok account, Shorts by Me may use information provided through TikTok's developer services to authenticate your account and perform actions that you authorize, including content uploading or publishing when the relevant permissions are available.

3. Video Content

Video files processed by Shorts by Me are used to create and prepare short-form content and to perform publishing actions requested by the user.

4. Use of Information

Information is used only to provide, operate, maintain, and improve the features of Shorts by Me, including authentication, video processing, and authorized social media publishing.

5. Data Sharing

Shorts by Me does not sell personal information. Information may be sent to connected platforms or service providers when necessary to provide a feature that you have requested or authorized.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

Shorts by Me may interact with third-party services such as TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, GitHub, or other services used by the application. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using Shorts by Me and revoke connected platform permissions through the relevant third-party platform.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Shorts by Me, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with this project.
