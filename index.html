Shorts by Me

Shorts by Me is an AI-powered video creation tool that helps creators turn long-form videos into short-form content and publish content to supported social media platforms.

What we do

Shorts by Me helps creators create short videos, prepare captions and metadata, and manage publishing workflows for social media.
